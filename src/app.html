How to use this site6 steps
  1. Get the book. This site uses the review questions from OCA: Oracle Certified Associate Java SE 8 Programmer I Study Guide, Exam 1Z0-808 by Jeanne Boyarsky and Scott Selikoff (Sybex/Wiley, ISBN 978-1-118-95740-0). You need your own PDF copy. Buy it from Wiley, or read it on O'Reilly (often free through a company or university account).
  2. Load the PDF. On this page, drag the PDF onto the box or click Choose PDF. It takes about a minute. The app finds the 145 chapter review questions and the answers and explanations from Appendix A. The file never leaves your browser.
  3. Practice. Open Practice, pick chapters, and choose all questions, ones you haven't answered yet, or ones you missed last time. After each answer you see the correct options and the book's explanation. Keyboard: A–G pick an option, Enter check or go to the next question.
  4. Take a mock exam. In Exam, choose 20, 40, 70 or all questions. The timer follows the real exam (70 questions in 150 minutes) and you need 65% to pass. Flag questions to come back to, then submit to see your score by chapter and review every answer.
  5. Review with flashcards. Flashcards has two decks: concept cards about exam traps, and the book's questions as flip cards. Tap a card or press Space to flip it. Press Got it to move a card up a box (3 boxes = mastered) or Again to see it again soon.
  6. Track your progress. Progress shows your accuracy per chapter and how many cards you've mastered. Everything is saved in this browser only, so use the same browser on each device. Clearing site data or using a private window means loading the PDF again.
